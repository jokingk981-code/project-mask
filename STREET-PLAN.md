BUILD 043 DESIGN

Dense London-inspired dystopian town. No uninterrupted straight road. Curved interlocking loops and short passages; 4.3–7.2m lanes; small irregular public courts; narrow adjoining 4–10m building frontages with depths 6–12m and heights 8–22m; almost no gaps between adjoining terraces. Routes protected from overlapping building footprints. Collision uses rotated footprints.

Source: Build 042. Existing Hall and outdoor transitions retained. Visual fidelity and route navigation require device testing.
