# BUILD 045 — MASK CITY

Reference: user-provided surreal teal/amber masked dystopia.

Implemented as actual Three.js geometry:
- Five Gothic spired skyline towers, emissive windows, roof bands and finials.
- Four suspended iron-and-bronze mechanical orbs above the city.
- Monumental sculptural face/crown outside the existing Hall landmark.
- Five rendered storefront signs, including MASKS, OPEN YOUR EYES and HALL OF MASKS.
- Thirty-six static masked citizen silhouettes across four market squares.
- Four overhead rigging/pennant assemblies.
- More teal/aged paving and stone materials.
- Existing winding streets, buildings, Hall interior, mask mechanics, controls and adaptive graphics retained.

Known limits: stylized low-poly geometry; not photorealistic, not animated citizens, no dynamic wet pavement/reflections. Actual on-device rendering and collision navigation untested. Source photo is a visual reference, not embedded as a fake 3D scene.
