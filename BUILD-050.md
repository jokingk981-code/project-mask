# PROJECT MASK — BUILD 050 / MASK MARKET CRAFT

Real game geometry additions:
- Seven three-dimensional mask merchant stands, with approximately 56 sculptural display masks.
- Each mask includes a face, deep eye sockets, cheeks, nose, and mouth; variations in ivory, bone and oxidized metal.
- Distinct rooftop attic/dormer additions and chimney caps on a subset of buildings.
- Sixty instanced street-edge rubble fragments with no additional individual draw calls.
- Existing skyline, Hall of Masks, masked figures, street lighting, wet cobbles, material maps and graphics settings preserved.

Validation: all inline scripts compiled together with node --check, ZIP CRC verified.
Limitations: not photorealistic; no full GPU/browser test, and no frame-rate guarantee.
